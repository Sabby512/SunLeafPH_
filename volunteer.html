Join as a Volunteer See activities
What volunteers can do

Your time and skills can spark change.

You do not need to be an expert. Students and community members can all help clean-energy awareness grow.

Prepare educational materials

Help design posters, slides, and simple guides that explain renewable energy.

Support school activities

Help run talks, quizzes, and energy-saving challenges in classrooms and clubs.

Share reliable information

Pass on clear, accurate facts about clean energy with friends, family, and online.
