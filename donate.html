Here is how your support can help the campaign.

Educational materials

Ways to give

Choose how you want to support us.

Pick one of the three options. Each one shows you what happens next before you do anything.

1

Donate via E-Wallet

Prefer to pay with your phone? Choose an e-wallet and an amount in just a few taps. Every peso helps us make clean-energy learning materials and awareness activities.

2

Donate via Debit or Credit Card

Give by card to support the campaign’s learning materials and digital resources. In a real campaign you would be sent to a secure payment page, so we would never see your card details.

Organize a Local Fundraiser

Rally your classmates or neighbors to raise funds for the campaign. A fun run, a bake sale, or a creative school event can all help spread the word about clean energy.
